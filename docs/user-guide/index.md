# User guide

These guides assume you have run the [tutorial](../getting-started/quickstart.md).

- [Combining datasets for a country](country-datasets.md). WorldPop population, Google and Microsoft building footprints, and OpenStreetMap places on an H3 grid or as tables.
- [Points and nearest buildings](points-and-buildings.md). Your own points, Giga school locations, and country building footprints.
- [Population around points](population-accessibility.md). Sum WorldPop inside a radius around schools, clinics, or other points.
- [OSM points of interest](osm-places.md). A fetcher for OpenStreetMap points of interest.
- [Road networks](road-networks.md). OSM roads around schools, routes, and the roads that connect them.
- [Processing a raster or a table](processing.md).
- [Settlement](settlement-characterization.md). GHSL settlement class.
- [Infrastructure files](infrastructure-normalization.md). Partner files, not a published dataset.
